Why clean up

Keep only the card data you need

Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard (PCI DSS) v4.0.1, Requirement 3.2.1
  • Limit the amount of data stored, and how long, to what the business needs.
  • Set specific retention requirements for stored account data.
  • Delete securely when the data is no longer needed.
  • Check at least every three months that data past its retention period is gone.
Paraphrased from the standard; check the exact wording against the published text before quoting it outward.

Where we stand

Storing cards is allowed. Storing cards with no business reason is not.
  • 525,111 stored cards have no subscription, no use in 3 months and no retention reason on record.
  • A written retention period for stored cards: NOT ESTABLISHED.
  • Every stored card is a liability in a breach; 525,000 of them buy nothing.
  • The in-house vault programme will move the vault: 128,000 cards move faster and cheaper than 679,000.
